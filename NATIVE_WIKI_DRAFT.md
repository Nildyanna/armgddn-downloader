## 1. What ARMGDDN Browser Is

ARMGDDN Browser is one app for finding and downloading everything in the ARMGDDN library: PC games, PCVR games, Testing, 3D printer models, Pirated PC apps, Comics and Music. You browse, pick, and press Download. The app downloads in the background and keeps going while you do something else. There is no separate website to open and no second app: browsing and downloading are the same window.

## 2. Getting Started

### 2.1 Install

Download ARMGDDN Browser for Windows, Linux or macOS 13 or newer from the Releases page (or the Download App button on the website). Run it once so it can finish setting itself up.

### 2.2 Log in

Sign in with Telegram when the app asks, and approve the login there. You stay logged in on that computer.

### 2.3 First look

The window opens on Home. Everything else is one click away in the bar across the top. Linux and Steam Deck players: see section 13.

## 3. Finding Your Way Around

### 3.1 The top bar

Home, PC Games, PCVR Games, Testing, 3D Printer Models, Pirated PC Apps, Comics and Music are on the left. Tools, Settings, Wiki, My List, Requests and Downloads are on the right. The page you are on is highlighted.

### 3.2 The second bar

Each page has its own controls here: a Back button, the page name, then search, category, sort and so on where they apply.

### 3.3 Back

Every screen has a Back button. Alt+Left and your mouse's back button work too.

### 3.4 Grid or list

The Grid / List switch on the right of the second bar shows titles as cards with art, or as a plain list. The app remembers your choice.

### 3.5 The A-Z bar

On long lists, the letter strip at the side shows only the titles that start with that letter. Click the letter again to bring the whole list back.

## 4. Home

- **Trending.** The strip of what's being downloaded most right now (7 and 30 days). Click a title to open it.
- **Game Radar.** Picks chosen from what you opened, saved and downloaded. Refresh for new ones, dismiss any you don't want, reset to bring them back.
- **Resume.** Takes you back to the folder you were in last time.

## 5. Browsing and Searching

- **Open a section** from the top bar. If a section has only one folder inside, the app opens it for you. A section whose download quota is used up is dimmed and can't be opened; quota usually resets within 24 hours.
- **Folders that point elsewhere.** A game folder that is almost empty is only a pointer to the other server. It says "You must download from PCVR server" (or PC server) with a Go there button that takes you to the right section.
- **Search** filters the page you are on. Punctuation and spacing are ignored, so "spider man" finds "Spider-Man".
- **Search everywhere.** Tick Search everywhere to search every section at once.
- **Mistyped a name?** If nothing matches exactly, the app shows the closest titles ("No exact match, closest:"). It forgives one slip in each word of five or more letters.
- **Also in another section.** When you search inside one section and the same title is in another (a newer build in Testing, for example), a line under the search says so, with a Search all sections button.
- **Newest and Older.** When the same game is in more than one place with different builds, the results are tagged Newest or Older so you don't have to compare version numbers.
- **Tell me when it is added.** Under any search, this button puts the title on your watchlist. You get a Telegram message when it is added to the library, or when a newer build of it appears. My watchlist shows what you're watching, and you can stop watching any title there. It is the same list the website uses.
- **Category / genre.** Where a section has them, the dropdown narrows the list (for example Music genres). Choose No genre to see titles not yet tagged.
- **Sort** by Name, Popularity, Added or Released, and press the reverse button to flip the order.
- **Save this list.** Saves the section's titles and sizes as a file you can open in Excel.

## 6. A Game's Page

Click a title for its details: the cover, Watch trailer, Read README, Browse files, Add to My List and Download.
- **Art.** Titles show their cover. Where a title has none yet, it shows its category's art until real art is added.
- **README.** Some games show their README before downloading. Use Copy PW for the archive password.
- **Browse files.** Look inside a game's folder and download single files.

## 7. Downloading

### 7.1 Start

Press Download on a game, album, comic collection or 3D model. The download starts straight away.

### 7.2 Several at once

Tick Pick several items, tick the titles you want, and download them together.

### 7.3 Update patches

If a game has an Update Patch, the app asks whether you already have the old repack. Yes downloads only the patch, No downloads only the full repack, Cancel stops.

### 7.4 The Downloads page

Shows every download with its progress, speed and time left. Each has Pause, Resume, Cancel or Retry, and Open folder when it's done. Clear history removes finished ones from the list.

### 7.5 Updating a game you already have

In Settings, Your games, Check my games lists which of your downloaded games have a newer version. Click one to open it in the library.

## 8. Music and Comics

- **Music** is by artist and album. The genre dropdown narrows the list.
- **Comics** are by series and issue. Where a comic's details have been checked, it shows under its proper title with its issue number and release date, and series are listed in release order. A ComicVine tag next to it opens that issue on ComicVine.
- **Publisher collections.** At the top of the Comics page, just under the Komga box, each publisher's collection (Marvel, DC and more as they are added) has a Download All button that downloads every series of it that has been copied so far.
- **Only what I'm missing.** Already have part of the collection? In Tools, scan your Comics or Music folder; the app then shows what you don't have.
- **Komga.** The Comics page has a Komga box with links to install it (section 12).
- **Folders.** Where Comics and Music download is set in Settings, Folders.

## 9. My List and Requests

- **My List** holds what you saved for later. Press the heart on any card.
- **Requests.** Ask for a game that isn't in the library, and read the replies. When requests are closed, the page says so.

## 10. Settings

Settings is in groups, one card each.
- **Updates:** Check for updates (turns into Update to vX when there is one), What's New, Update automatically.
- **Folders:** Download, Comics and Music folders.
- **Downloads:** how many at the same time, connections per file (leave on Automatic), speed limit, check files after downloading, extract .7z files automatically, the video on opening .7z files, and the download schedule (pause or slow downloads at set times).
- **Your games:** Check my games.
- **The app and its window:** use this window when the app starts, notifications, minimize to the tray, keep running in the tray when closed, start with the computer, start minimized, the login animation, and a way back to the classic window.
- **Import:** bring over the settings of an installed classic Companion, or your website preferences (My List, theme, hidden sections, recent searches).
- **Help and reports:** send error reports, and Help with problems (section 11).

## 11. Updates, Problems and Help

### 11.1 Updates

The app checks once an hour. When there is a new version a bar appears under the header with Update now. You can also press File > Check for updates. If downloads are running, finish or pause them first, because updating restarts the app.

### 11.2 What's New

Settings, Updates, Show what's new.

### 11.3 Help with problems

Settings, Help and reports: run a check, test your connection speed, copy a report, or send your log to the chat. Help > Open the log folder opens the log files.

### 11.4 Exit

File > Exit closes the app completely. Closing the window may keep it running in the tray, depending on your setting.

## 14. FAQ

- **Do I still need the Companion?** No. ARMGDDN Browser is both.
- **Can I go back to the old window?** Yes. File > Switch to the classic window, or Settings, The app and its window.
- **Where are my downloads?** Settings, Folders. Open folder on the Downloads page takes you there.
- **A page froze or shows an error.** Press Try again or Back. It is written to the log, and Help with problems can send it.
- **Where is the admin panel?** On the website, for staff.
