The fonts in this folder are the Google Fonts families that the website's themes use, bundled so the themes look the same offline.
Each family is published by its authors under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org), except where Google Fonts lists
Apache License 2.0 (the same permissive terms for use and redistribution). Families: Audiowide, Barlow Condensed, Chakra Petch, Cinzel,
Cormorant Garamond, DM Serif Display, EB Garamond, Figtree, Fredoka, IBM Plex Mono, JetBrains Mono, Karla, Lora, Manrope, Nunito, Orbitron,
Outfit, Playfair Display, Press Start 2P, Quicksand, Rajdhani, Righteous, Share Tech Mono, Sora, Space Grotesk, Teko, VT323.
Source of every file: https://fonts.google.com (latin subset, woff2), fetched by scripts/get_fonts.py. See each family's page there for its
copyright line and license text.
